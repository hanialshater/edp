Solving classical problems when you don't know the numbers

A side project of the WPO-Gym / EDP research. Six posts on what happens to the textbook algorithms when their inputs are hidden — and what changes when you stop pretending you know nothing about the problem.

The arc, in one sentence

Bandits are great when you genuinely know nothing about the problem; they are an expensive way to learn things a domain expert already knows. The series walks from "I know nothing" (Post 1) to "I know quite a lot and I keep learning the rest" (Post 6), and the move that connects them — replacing exploration with authored, editable policy — is what the paper calls EDP.
